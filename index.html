Postdoctoral Research Associate
College of Mathematics, University of Edinburgh

I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the ProbAI Hub, working with Dr Sara Wade. My research develops scalable probabilistic inference for high-dimensional problems.

Previously, I completed my PhD in Information Engineering at the University of Cambridge, advised by Prof Simon Godsill, working on Bayesian multi-object tracking, sensor fusion, and decentralised inference.

Research Interests

I work across probabilistic AI, statistics, and signal processing. My research focuses on developing scalable, uncertainty-aware inference methods, with applications to complex sequential and multi-agent systems.

  • Predictive and post-Bayes inference: martingale posteriors, predictive resampling, and generalised Bayes methods for robust uncertainty quantification under model misspecification.
  • Nonparametric Bayesian methods: Bayesian clustering and analysis for high-dimensional data, including biomedical applications such as RNA sequencing.
  • Scalable and distributed inference: variational inference, sequential Monte Carlo, and MCMC for high-dimensional latent-variable models.
  • Sensor fusion and multi-object tracking: decentralised optimisation and data fusion of heterogeneous sensors; robust tracking under clutter and occlusion; group interaction modelling and intent inference in multi-agent systems.

I actively seek interdisciplinary collaborations, particularly in applications such as animal interaction and behaviour learning, pollution tracking, and marine monitoring.

University of Cambridge, Department of Engineering

My PhD thesis studied Bayesian state-space models and efficient sequential inference methods for high-dimensional sequential tracking data. The work demonstrated how Monte Carlo sampling techniques can be made computationally practical for various large-scale tracking tasks.
